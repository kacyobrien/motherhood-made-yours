Motherhood Made Yours Community

Go a little deeper, together.

A space for women learning to trust their own voice, share honestly, and grow stronger together.

A space to grow together

What becomes possible when you have a place to bring your whole self?

This is an invitation to step into a space held for you and for other women on a similar journey. You can come with the questions you are living, the things you are learning, and the parts of yourself you are ready to know more deeply.

I want this to be a room we each bring something to. We listen, share what we have lived, and make space for someone else to do the same. The more honestly we show up, the more we can grow together.

Inside the community

Space for the inner journey, in real life.

Through our calls, the practices we return to, and the things we share between them, we can look at our lives with more honesty. This is where women from all walks of life bring their own experience, learn from one another, and choose what they are ready to heal or unlearn.

Time together

Two calls each month. One to slow down with guided reflection or meditation, and one for coaching, questions, and support.

Something to carry with you

A weekly question to sit with in your own way, and a small collection of meditations to return to when you need a moment.

Women who are here with you

A place to share what you are learning and hear from women doing their own inner work, without having to perform or have it all figured out.

“I always feel validated, I feel seen, I feel truly heard.”
— Abby
Our intention

Raising the consciousness of motherhood, one conversation at a time.

This is not a room for deciding whose way of mothering is right. It is a place to look within, to hear yourself more clearly, and to grow alongside women who are doing the same. We will share, connect, and make something together from all that we each bring.

There is room to speak honestly about hard things without staying stuck in them. We can choose to heal, unlearn, and take the next step in our own lives, with care for one another along the way.

An invitation

I'd love to welcome you in.

If the inner journey of motherhood speaks to you, I'd love to invite you in. Come as yourself, ready to contribute, listen, and grow with women who will do the same.
