What it looks like

The work is the inner journey.

Motherhood is messy. And I'm not just talking about your kitchen.

I have been coaching women for over a decade, through every kind of season and change. The women I work with come from all walks of life - different careers and different professions. And being a stay-at-home mom is absolutely a full-time career. The work meets on the level of motherhood. Whatever challenge or opportunity is in front of you, we look at the question underneath it - who do you need to be here?

We meet one-on-one over video and begin wherever you are that day. There is space to slow down, say the thing out loud, and listen for what feels true. No judgment, no rushing, no ten-step program. I draw on simple tools I practise myself, always in service of helping you hear yourself, know what is true for you, and become who you need to be.

We start with a relaxed thirty-minute chat to meet each other. If it feels right, we keep going from there and shape the work around your season and your real life (nap schedules included).

Questions, answered

Before we talk, you might be wondering...

What happens in an alignment chat?

It is a relaxed 30-minute conversation, not a sales script. You can tell me what has been feeling heavy, unclear, or ready to change. I will listen, ask a few honest questions, and we will see whether working together feels right for both of us.

Is this therapy?

No. I am a coach, not a therapist, and our work is not a replacement for mental-health or medical care. Coaching gives us a place to slow down, notice what is keeping you stuck, and make room for choices that feel more like your own. If what you need is outside my scope, I will say so plainly.

Do I have to be a certain kind of mother?

No. There is no mold to fit and no right way to do this. What matters is that you want to hear yourself more clearly and make choices that feel true for you and your family.

What does coaching cost?

The alignment chat is free. If we both feel there is a fit, I will walk you through the coaching options and cost before you decide anything. There is no pressure to commit on the call.

What tools do you teach?

Simple practices I use in my own life. They are clarity tools, never homework for its own sake. They help you hear yourself, know what is true for you, and become who you need to be - in motherhood and beyond it.

What if I am not sure I am “your person”?

You do not need to arrive certain. If you have spent so long listening to everyone else's shoulds that you can barely hear your own voice, we already have somewhere honest to begin. The chat is simply a chance to meet and find out whether this feels like a place you can exhale.
