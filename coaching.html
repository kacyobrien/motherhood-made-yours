What it's like to work with me

Space to hear yourself

This is a safe place to say the things you haven't said out loud. I'll listen to all of it, and I'll meet it with love. I'll also be honest with you, because you came here to grow, and I believe in who you're becoming.

My job isn't to hand you answers. It's to help you hear your own - and trust them. We go at your pace. Nothing to perform, nothing to prove.

One-on-one, at your pace

We meet one-on-one over video and begin wherever you are that day. There is space to slow down, say the thing out loud, and listen for what feels true. No judgment, no rushing. I draw on simple tools I practise myself, always in service of helping you hear yourself, know what is true for you, and become who you need to be.

A place to begin

We start with a relaxed thirty-minute chat to meet each other. If it feels right, we keep going from there and shape the work around your season and your real life.

Questions, answered

Before we talk, you might be wondering...

What happens in an alignment chat?

It is a relaxed 30-minute conversation, not a sales script. You can tell me what has been feeling heavy, unclear, or ready to change. I will listen, ask a few honest questions, and we will see whether working together feels right for both of us.

What if I am not sure coaching is right for me?

You do not need to arrive certain. If you have spent so long listening to everyone else's shoulds that you can barely hear your own voice, we already have somewhere honest to begin. The chat is simply a chance to meet and find out whether this feels like a place you can exhale.

What does coaching cost?

The alignment chat is free. If we both feel there is a fit, I will walk you through the coaching options and cost before you decide anything.

What tools do you teach?

Simple practices I use in my own life. They are clarity tools, never homework for its own sake. They help you hear yourself, know what is true for you, and become who you need to be.

What if I keep trying to control the outcome?

We're taught all these programs in life about how we should and shouldn't live, and that's a lot of where our control comes from. On this coaching journey, you'll learn to trust more and let go through the process.

Is this therapy?

No. I am a coach, not a therapist, and our work is not a replacement for mental-health or medical care. Coaching gives us a place to slow down, notice what is keeping you stuck, and make room for choices that feel more like your own. If what you need is outside my scope, I will say so plainly.

Do you work with teams and organizations?

Yes. I've worked with teams to help them find clarity and direction, and I've worked one-on-one with team members. I also do mindset coaching in group settings.
