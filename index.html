A little about me

Hey, I'm Kacy. Coach,
writer, mama, and your
biggest cheerleader.

Nearly a decade ago, I walked away from the path to medical school to follow my heart. It was scary, and it was right. Since then, I've had the honour of sitting with women as they learn who they want to be in the middle of real life.

This is my corner of the internet. Identity, relationships, healing, and the inner work of becoming. Whatever season you are in, the work comes back to learning to listen to yourself. You belong here and you are welcome here.
