A little about me

Hey, I'm Kacy. Coach,
writer, mama, and your
biggest cheerleader.

Nearly a decade ago, I walked away from the path to medical school to follow my heart. It was scary, and it was right. Since then, I've had the honour of sitting with women as they learn who they want to be in the middle of real life.

This is my kitchen table. Motherhood, identity, relationships, healing, and the inner work of becoming. If you're learning to trust yourself through all that motherhood brings, pull up a chair. You belong here.
